ffmpeg (bundled)
================

The desktop build of AD Patcher ships an unmodified ffmpeg executable
(imageio_ffmpeg/binaries/ffmpeg-win-x86_64-v7.1.exe, from the
imageio-ffmpeg 0.6.0 wheel). AD Patcher runs it as a separate program;
it is not linked into AD Patcher.
